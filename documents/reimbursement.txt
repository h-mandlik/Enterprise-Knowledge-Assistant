
EMPLOYEE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Policy Owner: Finance Department
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Submission Deadline
Employees must submit expense reimbursement claims within
30 calendar days of the transaction date.

2. Required Documentation
Every claim must include an itemized receipt, transaction
date, business purpose, and expense category.

3. Approval Process
The employee's manager must approve a claim before Finance
processes the reimbursement.

4. Missing Receipts
Claims without supporting receipts require Finance review.
Reimbursement is not guaranteed until the review is complete.

5. Processing
Finance reviews approved claims according to its internal
processing schedule.
